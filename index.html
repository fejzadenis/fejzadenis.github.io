Trading infrastructure · Quant research

BTC Binary Market Trading System

Strategy measured, then shelved on the evidence

A TypeScript and PostgreSQL system for Polymarket's BTC 5-minute up/down markets: discover the active market, capture near-expiry order-book snapshots, place and manage live orders, and settle on chain.

  • Built the whole loop: market discovery, a cron-driven collector, order placement through the Polymarket CLOB, open-position risk management, claim and redeem, and a backtester over captured snapshots.
  • Ran the validation myself: Brier scores, cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals on the residual, and breakeven half-spread against tick size.
  • The market midpoint turned out to be well calibrated and the edge did not survive execution costs, so I wrote that up and stopped trading it rather than shipping a losing strategy.
